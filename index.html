Personal music project

Music Analyzer

Music Analyzer is a personal, non-commercial Python project for organizing Apple Music playlist exports, identifying recordings, and enriching song metadata with tempo (BPM) and musical key information.

The project aims to assist in building playlists with smooth transitions, harmonic compatibility, and intentional energy progression.

Music metadata attribution

Musical tempo and key metadata may be provided in part by GetSongBPM. Visit GetSongBPM.com for BPM and key information.

This independent project is not affiliated with Apple or GetSongBPM.
